Disconnect STC Bookkeeping from QuickBooks

You can remove STC Bookkeeping's access to your QuickBooks Online company at any time:

  1. In QuickBooks Online, go to Settings › Apps › My apps, find STC Bookkeeping and choose Disconnect; or
  2. ask your bookkeeper at Simplified GST to disconnect it from our side.

When a company is disconnected, the access token is revoked with Intuit and the saved connection is deleted from our computer. Reports and working files already prepared for the client are kept under our client record-keeping obligations and are not used to access QuickBooks again.

Your connection has been disconnected if you arrived here from QuickBooks. Nothing else is needed.
