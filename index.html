STC Bookkeeping for QuickBooks Online

STC Bookkeeping is a private bookkeeping tool built and used by Simplified GST (Sales Tax Corporation) to maintain the QuickBooks Online books of its own bookkeeping clients. It is not offered to the public and is not listed on the QuickBooks App Store.

What it does

  • Reads reports, account histories and transactions from a client's QuickBooks Online company that the client has authorised.
  • Records journal entries, expenses, deposits and transfers that our bookkeepers prepare, after review.
  • Keeps a local log of every entry posted, for the client's audit trail.

How it is used

The tool runs on a computer operated by Simplified GST. A client's QuickBooks company is connected once by a company administrator through Intuit's standard sign-in, and the connection can be removed at any time from QuickBooks (Settings › Apps) or from our disconnect page.
