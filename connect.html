Connect your QuickBooks Online company

Connecting authorises STC Bookkeeping to read your QuickBooks Online data and to record the entries our bookkeepers prepare for you. Only a QuickBooks company administrator can authorise this.

  1. Your bookkeeper at Simplified GST starts the connection and sends you a secure Intuit sign-in link, or opens it with you.
  2. Sign in to Intuit, choose the company to connect, and click Connect.
  3. Intuit returns you to our confirmation page. The connection is then complete.

The connection lasts 100 days and renews automatically while the tool is in use. To reconnect after it expires, repeat the steps above.
