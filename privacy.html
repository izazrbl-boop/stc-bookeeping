Privacy policy – STC Bookkeeping

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains what STC Bookkeeping ("the tool"), operated by Simplified GST (Sales Tax Corporation) ("we"), does with information from QuickBooks Online when a client connects a company.

1. Who we are

Simplified GST is a sales tax and bookkeeping firm. The tool is used only by our own staff to keep the books of clients who have engaged us. It is not offered to the public.

2. What we access

When a client's QuickBooks administrator connects a company, Intuit gives the tool an access token for that company. With it the tool reads accounting data (chart of accounts, transactions, reports, lists of customers, vendors and classes) and creates or edits accounting transactions that our bookkeepers prepare for the client. We do not access payroll, payments or banking credentials.

3. How we use it

Only to perform the bookkeeping services the client has engaged us for: reconciliations, journal entries, reports and reviews. We do not sell, rent or share QuickBooks data with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Where it is stored

Access tokens, logs and exported reports are stored on a computer operated by Simplified GST, in a folder reserved for the tool and protected by the operating system's user login. Nothing is sent to any server other than Intuit's QuickBooks Online API. Reports prepared for a client are kept with that client's working papers for as long as we provide services and for the retention period required of accounting records, then deleted.

5. Who can see it

Only Simplified GST staff assigned to the client's engagement. We do not give other clients, contractors or outside parties access.

6. Security

Access to QuickBooks is through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 sign-in; we never see or store the client's QuickBooks password. The application secret is held in the operating system's protected credential store. Every write to QuickBooks is logged. Tokens can be revoked at any time (see Disconnecting).

7. Disconnecting and deletion

A client can disconnect the tool at any time in QuickBooks Online (Settings › Apps) or by asking us. On disconnection the token is revoked and the saved connection is deleted. On request we delete the exported reports and logs held for the client, subject to our record-keeping obligations.

8. Intuit

Use of QuickBooks Online is also governed by Intuit's own terms and privacy policy. Intuit is not responsible for this tool.
